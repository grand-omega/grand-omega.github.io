**Project Yuri** is the Grand Ωmega authorized-dealer storefront for
**Yuri Workshop**, an Italian luxury maker of fencing foils and blades.

The site is the dealer storefront — not the manufacturer's site — built
to sell Yuri Workshop equipment to the competitive-fencing community
under a Grand Ωmega-operated brand.

## Repositories

### [yuri-shop-site](https://github.com/grand-omega/yuri-shop-site)

The storefront. A full-stack Rust web app: server-rendered with
WASM-hydrated interactivity, a SQLite-backed product catalog, cart and
inquiry flows, and form validation.

**Stack:** Rust, Leptos, Axum, SQLx, SQLite, WASM.

## Status

**In development.** Catalog, cart, and inquiry forms are wired end-to-end;
brand identity and product photography are being finalized before public
launch.
